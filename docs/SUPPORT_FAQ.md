# Route Performance Tracker FAQ

## Do I need an account?
No for core local route tracking. An account is required for AI usage metering, cloud features, and subscription restoration.

## Does the app work offline?
Core local route tracking is designed to continue without internet. AI analysis, account actions, cloud backup/restore, and store verification require connectivity.

## Is this an Amazon app?
No. Route Performance Tracker is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon, any DSP, delivery company, or employer.

## How accurate is the finish forecast?
It is an estimate based on the route data and checkpoints available. It should become more useful as the route progresses, but traffic, access problems, rescues, weather, breaks, and other changes can move the actual finish time.

## What does Free include?
Core route tracking, checkpoints, delay/event logging, local route history, evidence photos, basic forecasting/stats, basic 7-day analytics, and 3 AI screenshot analyses per month for signed-in Free accounts.

## What does Pro include?
Expanded/fair-use AI analysis, advanced historical analytics, cloud backup/restore, deeper forecasting/difficulty insights, and enhanced reporting features as released.

## Are my evidence photos uploaded to the cloud?
Not in the initial release design. Route backup includes route/checkpoint/event information and evidence metadata, but original evidence image files remain local unless a future explicit evidence-storage feature is enabled.

## Why do I have to review AI-extracted route information?
Screenshot analysis can be wrong or incomplete. The app provides suggested values to reduce manual entry, not an authoritative copy of a delivery company's internal data.
