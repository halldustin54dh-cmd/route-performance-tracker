# Route Performance Tracker — Quick Start

## 1. Start your route
Open the app and create a route. Enter only information you actually know. You can also import supported route screenshots and review suggested values before using them.

## 2. Record the first stop
When delivery work actually begins, tap the first-stop action. This keeps loadout and travel-to-route time from contaminating your delivery pace.

## 3. Add checkpoints
During the route, enter your current stop number and save the checkpoint. The app records the time automatically and updates pace and forecast information.

## 4. Record meaningful delays
Use events for things that materially affect the route, such as access problems, wrong addresses, bathroom breaks, vehicle issues, weather delays, reorganization, or other interruptions. Add a reasonable delay duration when known.

## 5. Add evidence when useful
Photos and screenshots can be attached as route documentation or incident evidence. Evidence is not a substitute for required employer or official reporting procedures.

## 6. Review the forecast
After enough checkpoints exist, the app estimates the final-stop time using today's performance and available historical information. Forecasts are estimates, not promises.

## 7. Complete and review
Finish the route to add it to History and Analytics. Over time, the app becomes more useful because it can compare routes against your own prior performance.

## Accounts and Pro
Core tracking is local-first. Accounts support cloud features and subscription restoration. Free includes core tracking and a limited number of AI analyses. Pro adds expanded AI analysis, advanced analytics/forecasting, cloud features, and enhanced reporting.

## Safety
Do not interact with the app while driving. Enter information only when safely stopped and follow all applicable workplace and road-safety requirements.
