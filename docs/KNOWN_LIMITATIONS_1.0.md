# Known limitations — 1.0

- AI screenshot analysis requires a signed-in account and internet connection.
- Original evidence image files remain local and are not included in cloud route backup/restore.
- Cloud backup/restore is intentionally explicit/manual in 1.0 to avoid untested background conflict behavior.
- iOS/App Store purchase verification is not enabled in the Android-first release.
- Forecasts and AI-extracted values are estimates and require user review.
- Closed-test and Play production availability are controlled by Google Play review/testing requirements.
