# Account and Pro copy rules

Core route tracking does not require an account. Account prompts must only claim an account is needed for features that actually need identity/network state: AI usage, cloud features, and purchase restoration.

Free signed-in accounts receive 3 AI screenshot analyses per calendar month. Pro expands AI usage subject to fair-use/rate protection.

Cloud backup/restore and advanced historical analytics are Pro features. Free users retain local history, basic forecasting/statistics, and basic 7-day analytics.
