# Closed test — Day 1 checklist

1. Confirm tester opt-in link works on a non-owner Google account.
2. Install from Google Play closed track.
3. Confirm version/build number matches the intended release.
4. Create/sign into an account.
5. Test a Free AI analysis and verify quota usage increments.
6. Complete one full route workflow or demo route.
7. Test one Play test subscription and restore.
8. Confirm Pro advanced analytics/cloud actions unlock only after server verification.
9. Collect first usability feedback before recruiting the remainder of testers.
