# Store asset plan

## App icon
Direction: clean route/path motif combined with a checkpoint marker or performance pulse. Avoid Amazon branding, delivery-company logos, package-trade-dress imitation, or tiny text that disappears at launcher size.

## Phone screenshots
Capture production-style screens using demo/test data only. Do not use customer addresses or employer-sensitive screenshots.

Recommended sequence:
1. Today / active-route overview with finish forecast.
2. Multi-screenshot route import and AI review.
3. Checkpoint/event logging during a route.
4. Route History detail with documented delays/evidence.
5. Analytics showing Free basic summary and Pro advanced trends.
6. Account / cloud backup / Pro comparison.

## Screenshot captions
- Know where your route stands.
- Import route screenshots in one step.
- Track delays without rewriting your day.
- Build a record of what actually happened.
- Compare performance across real routes.
- Back up and unlock deeper analysis with Pro.

## Feature graphic
Use the app name with a simplified route visualization and three compact concepts: Track · Forecast · Document. Keep the graphic independent of any delivery company branding.
