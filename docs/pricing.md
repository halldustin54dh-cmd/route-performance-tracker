# Route Performance Tracker pricing

## Free

$0

Core route tracking remains useful without a subscription. Free includes route/checkpoint/delay logging, basic forecasting and stats, local history, evidence photos, and up to 3 AI screenshot analyses per month once server-side usage metering is enabled.

## Pro

- Monthly target price: **$6.99/month**
- Annual target price: **$66.99/year**
- Annual savings: approximately **20%** versus paying monthly for 12 months.

The annual plan must not be marketed as 50% off. Storefront prices are the source of truth and may vary by country, tax, and platform pricing tiers.

Pro is intended to include unlimited/fair-use AI route analysis, automated screenshot extraction, advanced forecasting/difficulty/history analytics, cloud features, expanded reporting, and performance comparisons.

## Store configuration

Product IDs:

- `route_tracker_pro_monthly`
- `route_tracker_pro_yearly`

Configure the corresponding Google Play and App Store subscription products as close as the stores permit to the target prices above. The app reads localized price and billing information from the store rather than hard-coding checkout prices.
