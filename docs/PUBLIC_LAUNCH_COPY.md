# Public launch copy draft

## One-sentence pitch
Route Performance Tracker helps delivery drivers track pace, forecast finish time, document delays and evidence, analyze route screenshots, and build a personal history of how real routes actually perform.

## Short launch post
Route Performance Tracker is built for delivery drivers who want a clearer record of their workday. Track checkpoints, delays, route difficulty, evidence, and finish forecasts; import route screenshots for assisted analysis; and compare routes against your own history instead of a one-size-fits-all pace target.

Free includes the core tracker and a limited monthly AI allowance. Pro adds expanded AI analysis, advanced analytics, cloud features, and enhanced reporting.

Independent tool. Not affiliated with Amazon, any DSP, delivery company, or employer.
