# Route Performance Tracker Privacy Policy — Draft

Effective date: To be set before public release.

Route Performance Tracker is designed as a local-first delivery productivity application. This draft must be reviewed and published at a stable public URL before production release.

## Information the app processes
The app may process account information such as email address; route information entered by the user; checkpoints, timestamps, delays, notes, route metrics, and performance history; screenshots and photos selected by the user; AI analysis results; cloud-backup metadata; and subscription/entitlement status.

## Local data
Core route information is stored on the user's device. Local tracking can continue without an account for supported core features.

## Accounts and cloud services
When a user creates an account or uses cloud features, account and backed-up route data may be stored using Supabase. Access controls are designed so authenticated users can access their own route records.

## AI screenshot analysis
When a user chooses AI-assisted screenshot analysis, resized analysis copies of selected images and relevant extracted text may be sent through Route Performance Tracker's secure backend to an AI service for analysis. Users should only submit content they are authorized to process. Original evidence files are not automatically uploaded as part of route cloud backup in the current release design.

## Payments
Subscriptions purchased in the Android app are processed by Google Play. Route Performance Tracker receives purchase identifiers/status needed to verify access but does not receive the user's full payment-card details.

## Data use
Data is used to provide route tracking, forecasting, analytics, backup/sync, AI analysis, subscription access, security, troubleshooting, and product operation. Route data is not sold to advertisers.

## Retention and deletion
A production release must provide clear account/data deletion instructions and define retention periods for cloud records and operational logs. Local data can be removed by deleting records in the app or clearing/uninstalling the application, subject to platform behavior.

## Security
The service uses authenticated access, server-side secret storage, database row-level security, and server verification for paid entitlements. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Children
The app is intended as a work/productivity tool and is not designed for children.

## Independence
Route Performance Tracker is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon, any DSP, delivery company, or employer.

## Contact
A public support/privacy contact address must be inserted before production release.
