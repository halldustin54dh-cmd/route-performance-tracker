# Beta feedback instructions

When something goes wrong, send:

1. What you were trying to do.
2. What happened.
3. What you expected to happen.
4. The approximate date/time.
5. Your phone model and Android version if known.
6. A screenshot or screen recording when safe and appropriate.
7. Whether you were online, offline, or had a weak connection.

For AI screenshot problems, also say which type of screenshot was involved (pick sheet, itinerary, route map, other) and which values were wrong. Do not resend sensitive customer information unless it is necessary and you are authorized to share it.

Useful non-bug feedback includes confusing wording, unnecessary steps, screens that feel crowded, features that are hard to find, forecasts that feel implausible, and things you expected the app to do but could not find.

## Suggested tester cadence

During the 14-day closed test, use the app in several meaningful sessions rather than only installing it. Delivery drivers should test at least one real route when practical. Non-drivers can still test accounts, screenshot import, navigation, backup/restore, history, subscription flows, and general usability.
