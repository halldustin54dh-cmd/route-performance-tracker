# Route Performance Tracker — Beta Tester Guide

## What the app does
Route Performance Tracker is an independent tool for delivery drivers. It helps drivers document a route, monitor pace, forecast completion time, record real-world delays and evidence, analyze route screenshots, and build a personal history of route performance.

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon, any DSP, or any delivery company.

## Basic test workflow
1. Create an account or sign in.
2. Start a route and enter the route information you know.
3. Import route screenshots when available. Review AI-extracted values before accepting them.
4. Record the first stop when delivery begins.
5. Add checkpoints during the route by entering the current stop number. Time is captured automatically.
6. Record meaningful delays/events and attach evidence when useful.
7. Finish the route and review the summary/history/analytics.
8. Test account backup and purchase restoration when instructed.

## What beta testers should try
- Account creation, sign-in, sign-out, and sign-in links.
- Starting, editing, completing, and reviewing routes.
- Screenshot import with one and multiple images.
- AI extraction accuracy for pick sheets, itineraries, and route maps.
- Checkpoint speed and finish-time forecasts.
- Delay/event logging and evidence photos.
- History and analytics with completed routes.
- Cloud backup and recovery behavior.
- Free/Pro messaging and test purchases when enabled.
- Offline use and returning to the app after losing connectivity.

## Useful feedback
Report what you were trying to do, what happened, what you expected, and a screenshot when possible. Also report confusing wording, unnecessary steps, incorrect extracted values, forecasts that feel obviously wrong, crashes, frozen screens, or data that appears to disappear.

Do not upload screenshots containing information you do not have permission to store or process.

## Closed-test participation
Please remain opted into the Google Play closed test for the full testing period. You do not need to use the app every day, but several meaningful sessions during the test are much more useful than installing it and forgetting it exists.
